# TASK-0061 Handoff

Implemented a versioned, synthetic-only pilot-capacity and environment-sizing review contract.

- Requires bounded tenant, concurrency, request, ingestion, and cell dimensions.
- Enforces controlled-pilot SLO thresholds, four saturation signals, owned scaling actions, immutable SHA-256-bound review evidence, and strict UTC RFC3339 review time.
- The evaluator always reports `NO-GO_EXTERNAL_PREREQUISITES`; it rejects any deployment and fails closed for incomplete, saturated, unowned, unreferenced, or altered evidence.
- Focused tests cover the valid contract plus dimensions, thresholds, saturation, evidence reference, digest, timestamp, and ownership failures.
